Our Policy

Due to the custom, made-to-order, and intimate nature of permanent jewelry, all sales are final. Because each piece is welded directly on your body and custom fit to you, we are unable to accept returns or exchanges once your jewelry has been applied.

Pre-Appointment Cancellations

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please contact us at least 24 hours in advance. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time may be subject to a cancellation fee.

Defective or Damaged Items

We stand behind the quality of our work and materials. If your jewelry breaks or becomes damaged within 30 days of your appointment due to a defect in our materials or workmanship, we will re-weld your piece at no charge. Please contact us with photos of the issue at concierge@latchtheory.com within the 30-day window.

Normal wear, intentional removal, loss, or damage caused by misuse is not covered under this policy.

Event Deposits

Deposits paid to reserve a Linked Event date are non-refundable. If an event needs to be rescheduled, your deposit may be applied to a new date at our discretion, subject to availability.
