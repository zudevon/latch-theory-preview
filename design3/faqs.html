Frequently Asked Questions

What is permanent jewelry?

Permanent jewelry is a piece of jewelry — typically a bracelet, anklet, or necklace — that is welded closed directly on your body with no clasp. It's designed to be worn indefinitely and won't fall off or get lost.

Does it hurt?

Not at all! The welding process uses a small pulse of electricity to fuse the chain together. You may feel a tiny bit of warmth, but most clients feel nothing at all. It takes just a few seconds.

How do I choose my chain?

We offer four chain tiers at different price points and styles. During your session, you'll be able to see and feel each option before making your choice. Our team will help guide you based on your preferences and lifestyle.

Can I shower, swim, or exercise with it on?

Yes — that's the beauty of permanent jewelry! Your piece is designed to be worn 24/7. You can shower, swim, sleep, and work out with it on. We recommend drying it gently after swimming in chlorinated or salt water to maintain its shine.

What if I need to remove it?

If you ever need to remove your piece — for surgery, travel, or any other reason — simply cut the chain with small scissors. It will not damage your skin. You're welcome to come back anytime to have it re-welded.

Do you offer events and parties?

Yes! We offer a range of Linked Events including private parties, girls nights, bachelorette experiences, corporate events, and more. Check out our Linked Events section or send us an inquiry for pricing and availability.

Where are you located?

We are based in Texas and travel to serve clients across the state. Contact us through the Let's Connect form to check availability in your area.
