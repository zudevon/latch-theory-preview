Frequently Asked Questions

What is permanent jewelry?

Permanent jewelry is a custom-fit piece — typically a bracelet, anklet, or necklace — that is welded directly onto your body with no clasp. It's designed to be worn continuously and removed only by cutting. And don't worry — we stand behind every piece we create. If your chain ever breaks due to normal wear, we offer free lifetime rewelds at any Latch Theory event, no questions asked.

Does it hurt?

Not at all! The welding process is quick, safe, and completely painless. There is no contact with your skin — only the chain ends are fused together.

How long does the process take?

The experience is quick and intimate — most appointments take 15 to 30 minutes depending on the piece and any customization.

How do I remove it if I need to?

Simply use a small pair of scissors or nail clippers to snip the chain. Because there is no clasp, it can be removed easily at any time — though we hope you'll keep it on forever.

Can I shower, swim, or work out with it on?

Yes! Your piece is made to live with you. Sterling Silver and Gold Filled are both durable enough for everyday activity. However, prolonged exposure to chlorine or harsh chemicals may affect the finish over time.

What metals do you offer?

We offer Sterling Silver and Gold Filled. Both are high-quality, skin-safe metals built to last through everyday wear.

Do you do private events?

Yes! We love being part of milestone moments. Whether it's a bridal shower, quinceañera, birthday, sorority event, or corporate gathering, we'd love to bring the Latch Theory experience to you. We are based in Austin and travel to all major Texas cities. Reach out through our website to inquire about booking.
