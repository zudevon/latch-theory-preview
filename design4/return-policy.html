Return & Reweld Policy

All Sales Are Final

Due to the custom, made-to-fit nature of our permanent jewelry, all sales are final. Each piece is individually welded to your body at the time of your appointment, making returns or exchanges not possible. We encourage you to take your time selecting your chain style and metal before your piece is applied.

Lifetime Free Reweld Guarantee

We stand behind every piece we create. If your chain breaks due to normal wear, we will reweld it for free — for life. Our reweld guarantee includes:

  • Free rewelds at any Latch Theory event, for the lifetime of your piece.
  • In-person only — rewelds are performed on-site at Latch Theory pop-ups and events.
  • Your customer information is securely saved at check-in for easy lookup.
  • Rewelds do not cover lost chains, theft, or intentional removal.

Latch Theory is based in Austin, TX and pops up at events across all major Texas cities. Follow us on social media or visit our website to find out where we'll be next.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out through the Let's Connect form on our website or via our social media channels.
